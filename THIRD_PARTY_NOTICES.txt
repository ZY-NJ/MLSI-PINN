FISSPy

The MLSI formulation and photospheric reference-line procedure implemented in
mlsi_pinn/physics.py and mlsi_pinn/velocity.py are based on FISSPy. The reference
inversions supplied with the training example were produced with FISSPy 1.0.0.
The differentiable forward calculation retains the research implementation's
Voigt approximation and is not the unmodified FISSPy solver.

Package: https://pypi.org/project/fisspy/
Project: http://fiss.snu.ac.kr/
